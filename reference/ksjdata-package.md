# ksjdata: National Land Numerical Information of Japan

Provides National Land Numerical Information (Kokudo Suuchi Joho)
published by the Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism
of Japan. A bundled catalog lists every downloadable file with its terms
of use; the selected files are downloaded and read on demand by
'targets' pipelines bundled with the package, and cached via
'tarchives'. Attributes are named after the official attribute table and
product specifications.
